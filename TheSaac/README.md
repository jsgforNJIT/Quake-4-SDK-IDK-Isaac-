So yeah here's a README: yeah basically i just use console commands to get/spawn stuff

How to download and play the mod:
1. Download the entire file from Github
2. Copy the TheSaac folder and paste it into the Quake 4 folder in C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Quake 4
3. On Steam, right click on Quake 4, select properties, and in Launch options, paste this shortcut: "+set fs_game TheSaac"

To see the new stuff, basically just use console commands (open with ctrl + alt + (~ or `)) to get the new weapons (give {insert weapon here, see list below})

Difference in weapons:
1. weapon_blaster: now has a six-shot clip of projectiles that needs to be charged to reload
2. weapon_shotgun and weapon_machinegun: now shoot projectiles
3. weapon_rocketlauncher: shoots projectiles rapidly, and with wpmod_rocketlauncher, rockets act like boomerangs and home in on the player
4. weapon_hyperblaster: replaced with a blaster that shoots grenades in a cluster with the number of grenades based on charge
5. weapon_nailgun: the first shot is a shotgun, then a regular sustained fire, and when it stops firing, fires a railgun shot
6. weapon_lightninggun: now has a small charge-up and tiny window to actually fire
7. weapon_railgun: replaced with a blaster that acts as a way to select powerups; regular fire to change which powerup is selected and charged fire to activate the powerup

Powerups selectable in weapon_railgun:
1. Vampire: gain quad damage and passive regen for a period of time
2. Invincibility: activate god mode for a period of time
3. Flying/Noclip: activate noClip for a period of time (if invincibility and flying are about to end, then the blaster starts emitting the firing effect)
4. Marine guy: spawns a random marine ally (Not yet implimented)
5. Teleportation: teleports player to where the player is looking (Not yet implimented)
